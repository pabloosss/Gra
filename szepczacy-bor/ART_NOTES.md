## Prompt tła — ImageGen

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background plate for an actual 2D pixel art woodland idle game, landscape 1536x1024. Create an original genuinely chunky, low-resolution 16-bit pixel art scene, as if drawn at 768x512 or lower with perfectly hard pixel edges and nearest-neighbor scaling, no painted blur. Twilight in a cozy mysterious Polish pine forest, subtle Lovecraft atmosphere. Restrained palette navy #111c27, deep forest green #193c36, moss #57705b, muted plum #574c65, brown #735444, tiny amber #efbd75. Composition for game: far layered conifers fill the upper 40 percent and left/right edges; a crescent moon and few pale stars in top center-left. The bottom 60 percent is a wide spacious mossy forest clearing with a gently winding brown dirt footpath across it and some small mushroom clusters, grass and stones at bottom corners. Keep center-left and most right-hand clearing OPEN and nearly empty so interactive tree, lumberjack and six village buildings can be placed on top by game engine. Soft pixelated mist at tree-line. One tiny dim turquoise unusual rune on distant trunk. No houses, no people, no large foreground tree in center, no interfaces, no text, no lettering, no watermark. Visually rich forest edges, quiet open clearing, cozy yet quietly uncanny. Actual reusable game background asset.

## Prompt atlasu drzew — ImageGen

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual 2D pixel-art game sprite atlas of THREE separate forest trees on TRUE transparent background. Layout: landscape 1536x1024, exactly three equal-width vertical columns (512 px each), one complete isolated tree in each column with large clear separation, all trunks ending on the same baseline near bottom with empty margin, no text. Left column a beautiful dense tall Scots pine/conifer with intricate asymmetric layered boughs, detailed clustered mossy needles and warm brown bark. Center column a gnarled ancient broad oak with craggy moss-covered trunk, dense foliage with large readable pixel clusters. Right column a mysterious subtly corrupted oak with muted grey-purple leaves, dark indigo bark, one small cyan branching rune in its trunk, a few small cyan lights between leaves, NOT monstrous, no faces or eyeballs. Palette strictly dark forest green, moss, navy, brown, muted plum with restrained light green highlights. Cozy twilight 16-bit pixel art matching a detailed atmospheric woodland game, hand-placed hard square pixels, NOT flat triangles, NOT blurry painting, NOT geometric icon. Render as low resolution sprite artwork around 150x220 logical pixels per tree with chunky visible pixels, nearest-neighbor pixel art look. Front facing slight top-down game perspective. Each entire tree is fully visible from treetop to roots and does not touch any other tree or cell boundary. No environment, no ground strip, no cast shadows extending beyond cell, no GUI, no letters, no border. True transparent RGBA background, not a checkerboard drawing. These three sprites will be individually extracted by column for use in Godot.
